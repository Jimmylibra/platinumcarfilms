1. Overview

We stand behind every roll of paint protection film we manufacture. This policy explains what is covered, for how long, under what conditions, and how to make a claim.

  • Up to 10 years coverage, depending on product line (see section 3)
  • Non-transferable
  • 24/7 claim support

2. What is covered

The Platinum Car Films warranty covers defects arising from the manufacturing process or the material properties of our polyurethane film. Coverage applies when the film has been purchased from an authorized distributor and installed by a certified professional in accordance with our installation guidelines.

Covered — manufacturing defects

  • Yellowing or discoloration caused by UV degradation of film material
  • Delamination, film separating from its backing or adhesive layers
  • Bubbling or blistering not caused by external mechanical impact
  • Cracking or splitting arising from material brittleness
  • Adhesive failure under normal driving and environmental conditions

Not covered — exclusions

  • Physical damage from rock chips, road debris, or vehicle collisions
  • Edge lifting caused by external factors
  • Scratches or abrasions caused by improper washing or detailing
  • Chemical damage from non-approved cleaning products or fuel spills
  • Staining from bird droppings or tree sap left untreated
  • Intentional removal or tampering by an uncertified technician
  • Normal wear and surface micro-marring from daily driving

4. Conditions to keep your warranty valid

  • Certified installation — installed by a certified PPF professional following Platinum Car Films installation guidelines
  • Approved maintenance — cleaned using pH-neutral automotive soap and soft microfibre cloths; avoid solvent-based cleaners, abrasive compounds, and automatic brush car washes within the first 30 days
  • Authorised product — applies only to genuine product purchased through an authorized distributor; grey-market film is not covered
  • Personal vehicle use — standard terms apply to personal and light commercial use; fleet and high-mileage commercial applications may require a separate written warranty agreement
  • Proof of purchase — a valid proof of purchase and installer certification record must be retained and provided at the time of any claim (digital records accepted)
  • Timely reporting — defects must be reported within 7 days of discovery; delayed reporting that results in further damage may affect claim eligibility

5. What voids the warranty

  • Non-certified installation — film applied without following the official installation process
  • Chemical contamination — exposure to solvent-based cleaners, fuel, brake fluid, or other harsh chemicals
  • Physical impact or collision damage — from road accidents, vandalism, deep rock chips, or external mechanical force
  • Removal by a non-certified technician — DIY or improper removal causing paint damage is not a warranty matter
  • Abrasive washing methods — automatic brush car washes, close-range pressure washers, or abrasive sponges
  • Non-approved overlay products — waxes, sealants, or coatings that cause chemical interaction
  • Untreated environmental contamination — bird droppings, tree sap, or industrial fallout left on the surface, causing etching
  • Expired warranty period — claims submitted after the product-specific period has elapsed
  • The warranty is non-transferable to a subsequent owner of the vehicle

6. How to make a claim

Most claims are resolved within 5–10 business days of receiving documentation.

  1. Document the defect — clear, well-lit photos from multiple angles, with a reference object (coin or ruler) for scale, noting when the defect was first observed
  2. Gather your records — proof of purchase, installer name/contact, and installation date
  3. Contact Platinum Car Films — via WhatsApp or through platinumcarfilms.com/contact-us/, including photos, purchase details, and installer record in the first message
  4. Claim review — reviewed within 3–5 business days; additional photos or a physical inspection by a certified installer may be requested
  5. Resolution — if approved: replacement film supply, re-installation credit, or a pro-rated refund based on the remaining warranty period at the time of the verified defect

Include in your claim message: product name and purchase date; installation date and installer name; clear photos of the defect; vehicle make, model, and year; description of when the defect appeared; your name and contact details.

7. Limitations of liability

This warranty is the sole and exclusive remedy for defects in Platinum Car Films paint protection film.

  • Remedy is limited to film or installation. Liability is limited to supplying replacement film, re-installation credit, or a pro-rated refund. We are not liable for repainting costs, vehicle storage, transportation, or consequential economic loss.
  • No warranty on underlying paint condition. This warranty covers the film itself, not the vehicle's underlying paint, clearcoat, or primer. Pre-existing paint defects visible through the film are not covered.
  • No consequential or indirect damages, including loss of use, depreciation in vehicle value, or costs incurred by third parties.
  • Governing jurisdiction — see flagged issue #5 above; this clause needs a specific, lawyer-reviewed answer before publishing given international sales.

8. Frequently asked questions

What does the Platinum Car Films PPF warranty cover?

Yellowing of up to 2% annually (considered normal and acceptable), bubbling or cracking from material failure, and adhesive failure under normal conditions. It does not cover physical damage, improper installation, or chemical misuse.

How long is the warranty on Platinum 210 PPF?

Platinum PPF products carry a 2–10 year manufacturer warranty from the date of professional installation, the longest in the Platinum Car Films range, reflecting the film's premium base material.

Does the warranty cover yellowing?

Every PPF film is prone to some yellowing caused by UV degradation; yellowing of up to 2% annually is considered a normal phenomenon. Platinum Car Films uses high-grade base materials and advanced manufacturing technology for UV stability and long-term optical clarity.

Is the warranty valid if I install the film myself?

No. The warranty requires installation by a certified PPF professional. DIY installation voids coverage, because improper surface preparation or technique can cause defects indistinguishable from material defects.

Can I use a ceramic coating over Platinum Car Films PPF?

In most cases, yes, applying a compatible ceramic coating over PPF is common professional practice. Only PPF-compatible ceramic products should be used. Consult a professional before applying any overlay product.

Is the warranty transferable when I sell my vehicle?

No. It is a non-transferable warranty.

Does the warranty apply globally?

Yes. The warranty applies globally to products purchased through authorized distributors. Claims can be submitted digitally or by email regardless of location.
