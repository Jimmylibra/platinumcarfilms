FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything B2B buyers ask before partnering with a PPF supplier.

Who is a paint protection film supplier?

A paint protection film manufacturer and supplier who provides PPF for automotive paint protection. The right supplier supports buyers with product options, technical details, customization, and sourcing confidence for professional PPF installation and resale.

How does Platinum Paint Protection Film work?

Paint Protection Film works as a transparent protective layer over vehicle paint. It helps reduce damage from light scratches, stone chips, stains, and environmental exposure while keeping the original paint finish visible.

How does PlatinumPPF work?

PlatinumPPF works as a transparent protective membrane preventing light scratches, stone chips, stains, and environmental exposure from deteriorating the original paint.

What is the difference between 7.5 mil and 8.5 or 9.5 mil PPF?

The difference is mainly film thickness and positioning. A 7.5 mil option may fit buyers who want balanced protection and price, while an 8.5 mil and above options may support buyers seeking a thicker premium protection story.

Is PPF better than PVC paint protection film?

PPF offers stronger elasticity, clearer finish potential, and better self-healing behavior than many PVC alternatives.

Do PlatinumPPF products are tested?

On request independent third-party lab reports, supplier material documentation, and real installation feedback can be provided.

Can PlatinumPPF be customized?

Is PlatinumPPF available for wholesale?

Yes. Ask for wholesale pricing directly from PlatinumPPF by sharing thickness preference, target market, order quantity, and branding needs so the team can recommend the right product option.

Do you offer global shipping supply support?
