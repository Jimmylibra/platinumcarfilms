0. This is a business-to-business policy

Platinum Car Films sells to installers, distributors, resellers, and other commercial buyers, not to individual consumers. This matters legally: EU consumer protection law, including the 14-day "cooling-off" right of withdrawal under the Consumer Rights Directive, generally applies only to sales to consumers, not to business-to-business transactions. This policy is written on that basis.

To keep that classification accurate in practice, not just in writing, CONFIRM the checkout/order process requires a business name and, where applicable, a VAT or business registration number before an order can be placed. Requiring this at the point of sale is standard practice for B2B-only sellers and gives the "business buyer" classification something concrete behind it, rather than just a policy statement.

1. Order cancellation

Orders can be cancelled for a full refund if the request is made before the order has shipped. Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled, see sections 2–3 below for what applies after that point. 2. Wrong or incorrect item received

If you receive a product that does not match your order (wrong thickness, finish, or product line), contact us at info@platinumcarfilms.com within [X days — CONFIRM] of delivery with your order number and photos of what was received. We will arrange a correction, replacement, or refund at no additional cost to you.

3. Damaged in transit

If a shipment arrives visibly damaged, document it with photos at the time of delivery and contact us within [X days — CONFIRM]. We will work with the carrier and arrange a replacement or refund for the affected items.

4. Product defects after installation

Defects in the film itself that appear after professional installation (yellowing, delamination, bubbling, adhesive failure, and similar issues) are handled under the separate Warranty Policy, not this refund policy. See that policy for coverage periods, exclusions, and the claims process.

5. What isn't eligible for a refund

  • Film that has already been installed, except where the Warranty Policy applies
  • Custom OEM/ODM orders where production has already started, unless the product is defective or doesn't match the agreed specification
  • Damage caused by improper storage, handling, or installation after delivery

6. How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method used for the order. CONFIRM typical processing time (e.g. "5–10 business days once approved").

7. International orders

For orders shipped outside China, including to the EU, any customs duties or import taxes already paid on a returned or cancelled shipment are generally non-refundable by Platinum Car Films, they would need to be reclaimed through the relevant customs authority, if that authority allows it. See the Shipping Policy for more on customs and duties.
