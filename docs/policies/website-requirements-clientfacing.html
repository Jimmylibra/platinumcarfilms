Website Requirements

platinumcarfilms.com — written to give the project an agreed baseline, since none currently exists

1. Purpose

This project has been running without a written requirements document, so there has never been an agreed, specific definition of what the finished site should include or how it should behave. This document is meant to fill that gap: a plain description of the pages, functionality, and content the site needs, so future work (fixes, edits, or new features) has something concrete to build against and check completion against.

3. Functional requirements

FUN-01Navigation

Header stays accessible while scrolling on both desktop and mobile. Mobile menu opens and closes reliably with no overlap or dead space.

FUN-02Forms

The quote/enquiry form, the Contact Us form, and the newsletter signup must all actually deliver their submissions, right now none of them do. Required fields are marked before submission, not only after a failed attempt. Validation errors name the specific field. One consistent design is used for the form everywhere it appears on the site.

FUN-03FAQs

Every FAQ/accordion item must open and close correctly, in both directions, on every page that has one.

FUN-04Gallery

Images are curated and captioned, with a working full-size viewer (not just a static grid), rather than every image asset in the media library dumped into one page.

FUN-05Search

Site search returns relevant matching pages/products for a valid query, and shows a clear empty state for no matches.

FUN-06WhatsApp / contact actions

The floating WhatsApp entry point, phone numbers, and email links all go to verified, correct destinations. One consistent phone number is used everywhere rather than two different ones.

4. No e-commerce/WooCommerce on this site

STRUCT-01StructureRemove the three leftover WooCommerce pages entirely

This is a B2B wholesale-enquiry site, not an online store, no product catalog, cart, or checkout should exist on it. /cart/, /checkout/, and /my-account/ are currently live, public URLs showing raw, unstyled WooCommerce shortcode output. These should be removed outright (not redirected or left in place): no e-commerce functionality, plugin, or leftover page should remain on the site. Wholesale enquiries continue to go through the Contact Us form as normal.

5. Content accuracy

These are inconsistencies between pages that should be resolved to one correct, consistent answer.

CONTENT-01ContentWarranty transferability is stated two different ways

The product FAQ sections and the Warranty page do not agree on whether the warranty can be transferred to a new owner.

CONTENT-02ContentHeadlight film's coverage duration does not match the Warranty page

The Headlight Paint Protection Film product page states a different warranty duration than the general Warranty page.

CONTENT-03ContentAbout page lists a different email than the footer/contact page

Only one email should be correct and used consistently sitewide.

CONTENT-04ContentGloss Black product page contains copy about a different finish

The comparison section on that page includes wording describing a satin finish, not gloss, likely copied from another product page and not fully edited.

CONTENT-05ContentPlatinum 210 page's thickness terminology is worth double-checking

It doesn't clearly match the standard phrasing used elsewhere on the site.

CONTENT-06ContentUnverifiable claims in marketing copy

Several pages include specific statistics, certification references, and pricing/financing language with no supporting source elsewhere on the site. Worth confirming each is something the business can stand behind.

6. Technical baseline

TECH-01Performance

No single image over roughly 300KB. No redundant libraries loaded twice for the same purpose (the site currently loads two slider libraries and two icon libraries).

TECH-02Mobile

All pages usable at common phone and tablet widths, with no oversized headings that wrap awkwardly and no fixed elements overlapping page content.

TECH-03SEO / metadata

Every page has an accurate title and description. Shared links show a preview image. No leftover internal/draft titles are exposed publicly.

7. Assumptions & out of scope

Assumptions

The site continues to run on its current hosting/platform unless a separate decision is made to change it. The business model stays wholesale/B2B enquiry-based, not direct-to-consumer online sales. Whoever implements these requirements has admin access to make the necessary page, plugin, and content changes.

Out of scope for this document

Hosting/infrastructure changes, a rebuild on a different platform or technology stack, new features not already part of the current site (booking systems, customer accounts, online payments), and marketing/SEO strategy beyond the basic metadata items listed above. Any of these can be scoped separately if needed.

8. Acceptance checklist

  • All required pages listed in section 2 are live and load correctly
  • Quote, contact, and newsletter forms all successfully deliver submissions
  • All four legal pages contain real, specific content
  • FAQs open and close correctly everywhere
  • Gallery is curated and captioned with a working viewer
  • No WooCommerce/e-commerce pages or plugins remain on the site (cart, checkout, my-account removed)
  • All content inconsistencies in section 5 are resolved to one correct answer
  • Site loads reasonably fast, with no unnecessary duplicate libraries
  • Site is fully usable on mobile
